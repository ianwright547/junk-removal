Piano and Pool Table Removal questions

Can you take an upright piano?

Yes, routinely. Tell us whether there are stairs and how many, because that changes the crew size significantly.

What about a grand piano?

Yes, but it needs a look first. Grands come apart for moving and the route out matters. Send photos and tell us the floor.

Do you disassemble a pool table?

Yes. Slate tables come apart into the frame, rails and slate sections, and the slate is the heavy part. It is the only safe way to move one.

Is the piano worth anything?

Usually less than people hope. Most used uprights have little or no resale value and many dealers will not take them. If yours is a recognised maker in good condition, get it appraised before disposal.

Can it be donated?

Sometimes, if it is in playable condition and holds tune. Schools and churches occasionally take them. A piano that has not been tuned in twenty years and sat in Florida humidity generally cannot be donated.

How much does piano removal cost?

More than a regular item because of the crew and equipment needed. Stairs and access drive the number more than the piano itself.

Can you get it out of a second floor?

Usually yes. Tell us before you book, because it changes how many people we send.

Do you take safes?

Ask first. Small safes yes. Large gun safes and floor safes are extremely heavy and may need specialist equipment.
