Exercise Equipment Removal questions

Will you take a treadmill apart?

Yes where needed. Most treadmills were assembled in the room and will not fit back out whole. We fold, dismantle and carry.

Can you get it down stairs?

Yes. Treadmills coming down stairs is a two or three person job and we bring the crew rather than risk the wall or the backs.

Do you take free weights?

Yes. Weights are dense so they fill the truck by weight rather than space. Give us a rough total so we quote it correctly.

Is it worth donating instead?

If it works, possibly, and we will tell you. Working cardio equipment can sometimes go to a donation partner. Broken equipment cannot be donated and a charity will refuse it.

Do you remove the gym flooring too?

Yes. Rubber tiles and rolled flooring, including adhesive-backed if it comes up.

How much does it cost to remove a treadmill?

A single treadmill is a small partial load. You will have the price before it moves.

Can you take a full home gym system?

Yes. Cable machines and power racks are bolted assemblies, so tell us what you have and we will bring tools.

Will you damage the doorframe?

We pad frames and corners and dismantle rather than force. If something genuinely will not fit without damage, we stop and tell you first.
