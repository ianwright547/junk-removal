TV and Electronics Removal in Orlando

Televisions, computers and e-waste taken to a recycler instead of a landfill.

Get a free quote Call (321) 364-4254

Electronics are one of the few categories where dumping genuinely matters. Old televisions and monitors contain leaded glass, and circuit boards contain heavy metals that should not go into the ground.

Most curbside services will not take a television at all, which is why so many of them end up beside a dumpster. We take them to an e-waste processor.

What we take

  • Flat screen and CRT televisions
  • Computers, laptops and monitors
  • Printers, scanners and copiers
  • Stereo and home theatre equipment
  • Game consoles
  • Phones and tablets in bulk
  • Cables, keyboards and peripherals
  • Office electronics in volume

TV and Electronics Removal questions

Do you take old tube televisions?

Yes. CRT televisions are heavy and contain leaded glass, which is exactly why they should not go in a dumpster and why many services refuse them.

Where do the electronics actually go?

To an e-waste recycler that separates the recoverable material and handles the rest properly. Not the landfill.

What about the data on my computer?

Remove or wipe the drive before we take it. We are a hauling company and do not perform certified data destruction. For business machines with sensitive data, use a certified ITAD provider instead.

Can you take a wall-mounted TV down?

Yes. Tell us it is mounted so we bring tools, and we can take the bracket too.

Do you take printers and copiers?

Yes. Office copiers are heavy and often need two people and a dolly, so mention the size.

Is there a fee for electronics?

E-waste usually carries a processing fee at the facility and that gets reflected in the price. Tell us the count when you book so the quote is right.

Can you take a working TV to donation instead?

If it works, say so. Working electronics can sometimes go to a donation partner, which is a better outcome than recycling.

Do you take batteries?

Small household batteries with a load, yes. Car and marine batteries, no, those need a dedicated drop-off, and most auto parts stores take them.

Can you clear a whole office of electronics?

Yes. Give us a count and we will bring the right truck and crew.
