Yard Waste Removal questions

Do you cut the tree down or only haul it?

Haul only. We take material that is already cut to manageable lengths. Felling a standing tree and grinding stumps is a tree service, and we will tell you that rather than attempt it.

Can you take palm trunks?

Yes, cut into sections we can carry. Whole palm trunks are extremely heavy and dense, so tell us the count and rough size when you ask for a quote.

Is this cheaper than waiting for county pickup?

No, county pickup is free. If you can wait and your material qualifies, wait. We are for when it does not qualify, when you cannot wait, or when it needs to come from the back yard rather than the curb.

Do you take it from the back yard?

Yes. Getting it to the curb is the part most people want to avoid and it is included.

What happens to yard waste?

It is typically composted or mulched rather than landfilled, which is one of the better outcomes in this business.

Can you take old mulch and sod?

Yes, but both are heavy and hold water. Tell us the volume, because heavy material is priced differently from light debris.

Do you take fencing and landscape timbers?

Yes, including posts. Mention concrete footings if there are any.

How fast can you come after a storm?

We will tell you honestly. After a major storm everyone calls at once and we will not promise a slot we cannot hold.

Do you take dirt and rock?

In limited quantities. Both are extremely heavy and hit the truck weight limit fast, so these are quoted by weight.
