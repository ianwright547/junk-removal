Hoarding Cleanout and Junk Removal in Orlando

Heavy-volume clearing handled without judgement, at a pace that works for the household.

Get a free quote Call (321) 364-4254

Let us be straight about what we are and are not. We are a junk removal company. We handle the hauling side of a hoarding situation: the volume, the sorting, the carrying and the disposal, and we do it without making anyone feel judged.

We are not a biohazard remediation company. If a property involves human or animal waste, decomposition, serious mould or a pest infestation, that needs a licensed remediation contractor before or alongside us, and we will tell you that rather than take the job and do it badly.

For the large majority of heavy-accumulation properties, what is actually needed is people, trucks and patience. That we can do.

What we take

  • Heavy accumulation throughout a property
  • Furniture buried under contents
  • Boxes, papers and bags in volume
  • Appliances and electronics
  • Expired food and packaging
  • Clothing and textiles in bulk
  • Yard and garage accumulation
  • Multiple truckloads over multiple days

Hoarding Cleanout questions

Do you handle hoarding situations?

We handle the hauling side: volume, sorting, carrying and disposal. What we do not do is biohazard remediation. If there is human or animal waste, decomposition, serious mould or an active infestation, that needs a licensed remediation contractor. We will tell you honestly which situation you have.

Will your crew judge the household?

No, and we take that seriously. Our crew works the job and does not comment. If the person living there is present, we follow their pace and their instructions about what gets looked at.

Can we go slowly, over several days?

Yes, and for most households that works far better than one overwhelming day. We can work in sessions, room by room, with breaks between.

What if the person changes their mind mid-job?

We stop. Nothing leaves without agreement. Forcing the pace damages trust and usually makes the overall situation worse, not better.

Do you look through things before disposing?

As much as you want us to. Some families want everything checked for documents and valuables, some want speed. Tell us which and we work that way.

How much does a hoarding cleanout cost?

It is volume work, usually many truckloads, so it is one of the larger jobs we do. We will not quote it without seeing it. A walkthrough costs you nothing and gets you a real number.

Can you work with a therapist or social worker?

Yes, and where one is involved it usually goes better. We take direction from whoever the family has coordinating.

Is it confidential?

Yes. Unmarked approach if you want it, no photos shared, no discussion of the property with anyone but you.

What about pests?

Tell us before we arrive. Light activity we can work around with the right protection. An active infestation needs pest control first, and we will say so.
