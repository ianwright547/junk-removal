Playset and Swing Set Removal in Orlando

Outgrown wooden playsets and trampolines dismantled and hauled, including the ground cover.

A wooden playset outlives its usefulness long before it falls down, and by the time the kids have outgrown it the wood has usually had years of Florida sun and rain.

They are bolted assemblies, often into concrete, and taking one apart safely takes tools and time most people would rather not spend.

Playset and Swing Set Removal questions

Do you take the playset apart?

Yes. They are bolted assemblies and we dismantle them into pieces that fit through the gate and into the truck.

What about the concrete the posts are set in?

We pull footings where we can. Mention them when you ask for a quote because they add time and weight.

Can you take the rubber mulch underneath?

Yes. It is heavy and holds water, so tell us roughly how much area it covers.

Do you take trampolines?

Yes, frame, mat, springs and enclosure. Anchored trampolines take a bit longer.

Can you remove an in-ground basketball hoop?

Yes. The pole is usually set in a substantial concrete footing, so that gets quoted as part of the job rather than assumed.

Is an old playset worth selling?

Rarely worth the effort once it has had a few Florida summers. Sun-damaged wood, faded plastic and rusted hardware sell for very little, and dismantling and delivering it for a buyer is most of the work.

How long does it take?

Most residential playsets are a half day including footings and haul.

Can you get it out through a side gate?

That is why we dismantle. Give us the narrowest gate width and we size the pieces accordingly.
