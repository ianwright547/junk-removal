Photo uploader

Add job photos to the Recent jobs section. Photos go live about a minute after upload.

Tap to choose photos or drag them here · up to 12 · JPG, PNG or WebP

    Photos are resized to 1600px wide and converted to JPG in your browser before upload, so big camera files are fine. iPhone HEIC files will not work — set Settings → Camera → Formats to Most Compatible, or export as JPG first.

    Slots fill in order. Uploading to a slot that already has a photo replaces it. The site hides the Recent jobs section entirely when every slot is empty.
