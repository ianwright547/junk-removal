Questions people ask before booking

How much does junk removal cost in Oakdale?

We price by how much of the truck your stuff fills. A single item starts around $99 and a full truckload runs about $649. Labor, loading, and disposal are included. Use the estimator up top for a quick range.

Can you pick up today?

Usually yes. Call before noon and we can often be there the same afternoon.

Do I have to carry anything to the curb?

No. Point at it and we carry it out from wherever it is: attic, basement, backyard, second floor.

Do I need to be home?

Not always. For curbside or garage pickups you can leave it out, pay online, and we'll send photos when it's done.

What happens to my stuff?

Usable furniture goes to local donation centers, metal and cardboard get recycled, and only what's left goes to the landfill.
