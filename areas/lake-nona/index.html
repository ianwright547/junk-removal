Lake Nona junk removal questions

Do you do same-day junk removal in Lake Nona?

Usually, yes. We are based on Narcoossee Road, so there is no cross-town drive to get to 32827 or 32832. Call before noon and same afternoon is normally doable. Later in the day we will tell you honestly whether it is today or first thing tomorrow.

My HOA does not allow anything at the curb. Is that a problem?

No, and it is the most common question we get here. Most Lake Nona communities have rules about what can sit outside and for how long. We do not stage anything at the curb. We carry it from where it is straight into the truck and leave with it the same visit.

Can you get into Laureate Park and Village Walk with a truck?

Yes. The alleys and rear-loaded garages are tighter than a standard street, so we park where we can legally fit and carry from there. Tell us if you are on an alley-load lot and we will plan for it.

I have a garage full of flattened moving boxes. Do you take cardboard?

Yes, and cardboard is one of the better outcomes. It is clean recyclable material, so it goes to recycling rather than landfill. Volume is what sets the price, and flattened boxes compress well, which usually works in your favor.

Do you handle apartment turnovers near Medical City?

Yes. Give us the unit, the access details and the date the unit has to be clear. We work turnover deadlines regularly and we will send photos when the unit is empty.

Can you take the builder debris left in my new home?

Leftover trim, packaging, drywall offcuts and the like, yes. What we cannot take is anything hazardous: paint, solvents, fuel, propane or chemicals. Those need household hazardous waste drop-off.

Will you take the appliance packaging and the old appliance?

Both. The packaging is bulky but light. The appliance itself we take too, and if it still runs we try to route it to a donation partner rather than scrap it.

Do I need to be home?

No. Plenty of Lake Nona jobs are handled with a garage code or a photo of what goes. We confirm by text before we load and send photos when we are done.

Is there a trip fee for Lake Nona?

No. Lake Nona is our home base, so there is no trip charge anywhere in 32827 or 32832.

Can you do a second-floor or third-floor unit?

Yes. Stairs are part of the job and we do not charge extra for them. Tell us the floor when you call so we bring the right number of people.
