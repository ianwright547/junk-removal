Waterford Lakes junk removal questions

Do you handle student rental turnovers?

Yes, and we know the calendar. End of semester is the busy window here and it books out, so call early rather than the week it happens.

Can you clear several units at once?

Yes. Give us the addresses, access details and the deadline, and you get photo confirmation per unit.

Do you take abandoned mattresses?

Yes, and it is the most common item in this area. Tell us the count.

What about tenant property left behind?

Florida law sets out a process for handling personal property left after a tenancy ends. Confirm the correct procedure with your attorney or property manager before anything is removed. We cannot make that call for you.

Is there a trip fee?

No. Waterford Lakes is about twenty-two minutes out.

Can you do a garage cleanout here?

Yes, and it is the other half of our work in this area. Send photos for an accurate number.

Do you work with property management companies?

Yes. One contact, multiple properties, documentation on each.

Can you come same day?

Sometimes, depending on the day. Outside the end-of-semester rush it is more likely.

Do you take electronics?

Yes. Televisions, monitors and computers go to an e-waste recycler rather than the landfill.
