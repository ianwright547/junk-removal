Celebration junk removal questions

Celebration has strict appearance rules. Is that a problem?

No, because nothing we do involves leaving anything visible. We carry from inside the property directly into the truck and leave with it in the same visit.

Can your truck get down the rear alleys?

Usually, with planning. Some lanes are tight. Tell us if you are alley-loaded and send a photo of the access if you are unsure.

Do you need to register with the town or the HOA?

For a normal removal, no. Anything involving demolition or equipment may require contractor registration, so check with the association first if your job involves a structure.

Is there a trip fee for Celebration?

No. It is about twenty-five minutes and pricing is the same as everywhere else.

Can you handle a townhome with narrow stairs?

Yes. Stairs are included and we bring enough crew. Tell us the floor and whether the stairwell turns.

Do you work with property managers on second homes?

Yes. Access details in advance, photos when done, no need for anyone to be on site.

Can you come on short notice?

We will try. Same-day this far out is not guaranteed, and we will give you an honest earliest time rather than a hopeful one.

Do you take patio furniture?

Yes. Sun-destroyed patio sets are one of the most common items in this part of the service area.

What cannot be taken?

Paint, solvents, fuel, propane, pool chemicals and pesticides. We will flag anything in your pile before quoting.
