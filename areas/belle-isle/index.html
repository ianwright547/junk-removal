Belle Isle is its own incorporated city rather than part of unincorporated Orange County, which matters more than people expect. It has its own rules about what can sit at the curb and for how long, and enforcement is real because it is a small city where things get noticed.

Our approach here is simple: nothing goes to the curb. We load from where the item sits and leave with it in the same visit, so there is no window where a pile is sitting outside your house.

The lake frontage on the Conway chain also means the usual waterfront accumulation, and the streets around Nela Isle and Venetian Gardens are tight enough that we plan the park before we arrive.

Belle Isle junk removal questions

Belle Isle has rules about curbside junk. How do you handle that?

By not using the curb at all. We carry everything from where it is directly into the truck and drive away with it. Nothing sits outside your house waiting for a pickup day, so there is nothing for the city to take issue with.

Should I check with the city before I book?

For a normal household removal, no, because nothing goes on public property. If a project involves demolition, a dumpster on the street or structural work, check with Belle Isle first, since those can carry permit requirements we cannot speak to on your behalf.

Can your truck handle the narrow streets around Nela Isle?

Yes, with a little planning. Some of those streets and driveways are tight, so we may park on the street and carry. Send a photo of the access if you are unsure and we will confirm before the day.

Do you take dock and waterfront material?

Yes, once it is out of the water. Decking, railings, covers, old lifts and shoreline junk. In-water removal needs a marine contractor, not us.

Is there a trip fee for Belle Isle?

No. It is roughly eighteen minutes from our base and pricing is the same as everywhere else we serve.

Can you do a whole-house cleanout?

Yes. Whole-house jobs usually take multiple truckloads, and we would rather walk it or see photos first and give you a real number than quote blind.

Do you take mattresses?

Yes. Mattresses are one of the worst things to leave at a curb and one of the more annoying things to dispose of yourself, which is why they are one of our most common single items.

What about paint and pool chemicals?

Neither, unfortunately. Paint, pool chemicals, solvents and fuel all need household hazardous waste drop-off. We will tell you what in your pile we cannot take before we start loading, not after.

Can you come on a Saturday?

Yes. We run Monday through Saturday, 7am to 7pm.

Do you give a price before you start?

Yes. You get the number before anything goes in the truck, and if the load ends up smaller than we estimated, the price comes down.
