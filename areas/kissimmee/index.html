Kissimmee runs on short-term rentals, and that creates a kind of junk removal work that barely exists elsewhere in our area. Vacation homes replace furniture on a cycle, guests break things, and a unit has to be guest-ready between bookings with no gap.

That means we schedule around check-out and check-in rather than around a normal workday, and we are used to property managers handling several properties at once.

Kissimmee junk removal questions

Can you work around guest check-in and check-out?

Yes, and this is most of what we do in Kissimmee. Give us the window and we will work inside it. If the window is tight, tell us up front so we bring enough crew to finish inside it.

Do you work with property managers on multiple units?

Yes. One contact, several addresses, and photo confirmation for each unit so you can prove the job was done without driving out to look.

Can you do a furniture swap the same day the new furniture arrives?

Yes, and timing matters. Tell us when the delivery lands so we clear the old set before it, not after. Otherwise you have two sets of furniture in one house and nowhere to put either.

Is there a trip fee for Kissimmee?

No. Kissimmee is about twenty-five minutes out and pricing is the same as everywhere else we serve.

Do you take pool and patio furniture?

Yes. Sun-wrecked patio sets are one of the most common vacation rental items we haul.

Can you handle mattresses in bulk?

Yes. Rentals replace mattresses on a schedule and we are set up for volume. Tell us the count and sizes when you book.

What if the unit has a gate code or lockbox?

That is normal here. Give us the access details and we will handle it without anyone meeting us on site. Photos when we are done.

Do you take TVs and electronics?

Yes. Electronics go to an e-waste recycler rather than the landfill. Older tube TVs are heavy and awkward but we still take them.

Can you come on short notice if a guest leaves a mess?

We will try. Same-day out to Kissimmee is not guaranteed because of the drive, but tell us what happened and we will tell you the earliest we can realistically be there.

Do you take construction debris from a renovation?

Yes, within reason. Drywall, trim, flooring, cabinets and fixtures. What we do not take is hazardous material, and if the property is old enough to have asbestos flooring it needs testing first.
