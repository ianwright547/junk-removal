Hunters Creek junk removal questions

My HOA does not allow items at the curb. How does that work?

It does not affect us, because we never use the curb. We carry everything from where it sits directly into the truck and leave with it the same visit. Nothing sits outside.

Do you need HOA approval to come out?

For a normal removal, no. Some communities require contractor registration for work involving equipment or demolition, so if your job involves a shed or a deck, check with your HOA first.

Can you take a wooden playset from the back yard?

Yes. We dismantle it, including the posts and footings, and carry it out through the gate. Give us the narrowest gate width.

Is there a trip fee for Hunters Creek?

No. It is about twenty minutes and there is no trip charge.

Do you take trampolines and basketball hoops?

Yes to both. In-ground basketball poles have a substantial concrete footing, so mention that when you ask for a quote.

Can you do a garage cleanout in one visit?

Usually. A packed two-car garage is most of a day and often more than one truckload, but we will tell you honestly when we see the photos.

Do I have to sort it first?

No. Point out what stays and we work around it. Sorting is why garages stay full.

Do you take exercise equipment?

Yes. Treadmills and home gyms are heavy and were assembled in the room, so we dismantle and carry.

What about paint and garage chemicals?

Not those. Liquid paint, fuel, propane, pesticides and pool chemicals all need household hazardous waste drop-off. We will point them out before we start loading.
