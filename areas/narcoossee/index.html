Narcoossee is a short run down the road from us, and the work here looks different from the subdivisions north of it. Lots are bigger, a lot of properties have outbuildings, and the junk is often outside rather than in a garage.

That means sheds that have collapsed or rotted, fence sections replaced after a storm, old above-ground pools, trailers, tractor parts and the general accumulation that happens when a property has room to accumulate.

Long driveways and gates are normal here. Tell us about them when you call and we will bring the right truck and plan the approach instead of discovering it on arrival.

Narcoossee junk removal questions

Can you get down a long dirt or gravel driveway?

Usually yes. What stops us is soft ground after heavy rain and low tree limbs. Send a photo of the driveway when you call and we will tell you straight whether the truck makes it or whether we need to shuttle from a firmer spot.

Do you tear down sheds, or only haul them if they are already down?

We take them down. Wood and metal sheds are routine. If the shed has a concrete pad, tell us up front, because whether the pad goes changes the job significantly.

Is there a trip fee out to Narcoossee?

No. We are about ten minutes away on Narcoossee Road, so the same pricing applies as anywhere else we serve.

Can you remove old fencing including the posts?

Yes, including posts set in concrete. Post footings are slow work, so tell us roughly how many sections and whether the posts are concreted when you ask for a quote.

What about an above-ground pool?

Yes. It needs to be drained before we arrive, and we will cut it down and haul it. If there is a deck or surround going too, mention it, because that is often the larger half of the job.

Do you take scrap metal and old equipment?

Yes. Metal is one of the things we genuinely want, because it recycles cleanly. Old mowers, appliances, fencing, bed frames and general scrap are all fine.

I have a gate with a code. How does that work?

Give us the code or the gate contact when you book. Plenty of our Narcoossee jobs happen without the owner on site.

Can you handle a full barn or outbuilding cleanout?

Yes, and these are usually a multi-truck job. We would rather look at photos or walk it first and give you a real number than guess and surprise you.

What will you not take?

Paint, solvents, fuel, pesticides, propane tanks, asbestos and anything else classed as hazardous. On rural properties old fuel cans and pesticide containers come up often, so it is worth checking before we arrive.

Do you take yard debris and tree limbs?

Yes, cut to manageable lengths. Whole felled trees and stumps are a different trade and we will tell you if you need a tree service instead of us.
