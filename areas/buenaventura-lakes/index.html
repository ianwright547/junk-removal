Buenaventura Lakes junk removal questions

Is there a trip fee?

No. Buenaventura Lakes is about twenty-two minutes from our base.

Can your truck fit on a narrow street?

Usually. Where driveways are short or streets are tight we park where we can legally fit and carry from there, and that does not change your price.

Do you take several items in one visit?

Yes, and it is almost always cheaper than booking separate trips. Tell us everything when you call so the quote covers it all.

Do you handle rental move-outs?

Yes. Give us the deadline and we work to it, with photos when the unit is clear.

Can you do same day?

Sometimes. It is a little further out than Lake Nona, so we will give you an honest answer rather than a hopeful one.

Do you take appliances?

Yes. Refrigerators and freezers go through a certified recycler so the refrigerant is recovered.

Do I need to be home?

Not always. Access details and a photo of what goes is often enough, and we confirm by text before loading.

Do you take yard waste?

Yes, cut to manageable lengths. Whole standing trees need a tree service.

What is the minimum job?

A single item. There is a floor because a truck still has to drive out, but one item costs a fraction of a full load.
