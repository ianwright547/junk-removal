Azalea Park junk removal questions

Can you handle a garage that has been full for forty years?

Yes, and it is what we do most here. Expect more than one truckload and let us see photos so the quote is real.

My house is from the 1960s. Is asbestos a concern?

It can be. Floor tile and the black adhesive under it, pipe wrap and some ceiling textures from that era may contain asbestos. We do not disturb or haul suspected asbestos material. It needs testing and a licensed abatement contractor first.

Do you take old tools and machinery?

Yes, if we can move it. Mowers, hand tools, workbenches and general metal. Large fixed machinery may need equipment we do not carry.

Can you get a truck down an older driveway?

Usually. If not, we park at the street and carry, and that does not change your price.

Do you clear detached garages and sheds?

Yes, both, and we can take the shed itself down if it is going.

Is there a trip fee?

No. Azalea Park is about eighteen minutes out.

What happens to the metal?

It goes to a recycler. Metal is one of the better outcomes in this business and a metal-heavy load can work in your favour on price.

Do you take tires?

Usually, but give us a count. Tires carry a per-tire disposal fee almost everywhere, and old garages often have more than the owner remembers.

Can you work with a realtor on a pre-sale clearout?

Yes. Tell us the listing date and we will work to it.
