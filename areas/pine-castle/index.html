Pine Castle junk removal questions

Do you do commercial units as well as houses?

Yes. Small commercial and light industrial units are routine here, including after hours if you need the business open during the day.

Can you clear a workshop?

Yes, including benches, racking, tools and scrap. Tell us if there is machinery that needs more than two people to move.

Do you provide a certificate of insurance?

Yes. Most commercial landlords require one, so ask early and give us the certificate holder details.

Is there a trip fee?

No.

Can you take fluids and chemicals from a workshop?

No. Oils, solvents, fuels and industrial chemicals need proper disposal through a licensed handler. We will identify what we cannot take before quoting.

Do you take scrap metal?

Yes, and gladly. It recycles cleanly and a metal-heavy load usually helps your price.

What about a residential estate cleanout?

Yes. We work at your pace, set aside anything you want kept, and can get you a donation receipt.

Can you come after hours for a commercial job?

Yes. Tell us the window and we will size the crew to finish inside it.

How is it priced?

By truck volume for general material, by weight for heavy material like concrete. You get the number before we load.
