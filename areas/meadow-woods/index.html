Meadow Woods junk removal questions

How quickly can you get to Meadow Woods?

Faster than almost anywhere we serve. It is roughly twelve minutes from our base, so same-day is realistic on most days if you call in the morning.

Is there a trip fee?

No.

Do you handle rental move-outs?

Yes. Give us the date the unit has to be clear and we work to it, with photos when it is empty.

Can you take a single mattress?

Yes, and it is one of our most common calls. Priced as a small partial load, taken from the bedroom not the curb.

Do you clear sheds?

Yes, including taking the shed down if it is going. Tell us if there is a concrete pad.

Can you come on a Saturday?

Yes. Monday through Saturday, 7am to 7pm.

Do I need to be home?

Not necessarily. A gate code, a garage code or a photo of what goes is often enough. We confirm by text before loading.

What does a garage cleanout cost?

By volume. Send photos of the garage and you will get a number that holds rather than a guess that climbs.

Do you take appliances?

Yes, including refrigerators and freezers, which go through a certified recycler so the refrigerant is recovered properly.
